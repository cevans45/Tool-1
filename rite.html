Rite

Rite

The frame is cut into blocks. Each block holds one word set to its edges, a sigil, a dropped image, a field of colour, or nothing. A seed is a whole composition, so reseeding is a cut — and the music decides when to cut.

Modes

1–6 switch how the frame is partitioned: Blocks, Stack, Column, Frame, Scatter, Solo. Content, motion and colour are shared, so the mode is just a different reading of the same machine.

Motion

The six dials are routings, 0–10. Each one says how hard a part of the music drives a behaviour, so you wire the track to the frame once and then leave it alone.

Training

T turns training on. Space keeps a composition, X rejects it. The generator then leans toward what you kept — density, chaos, ink and mode — without moving the sliders themselves.

Scenes

Ctrl+1–8 stores the current look. Shift+1–8 recalls it. Slots survive a reload.

Sigils

This is the twist. Draw a Sigil opens a surface over the stage. Anything you keep goes into the library and scratches itself into blocks over four beats. With an empty library the engine draws its own.

Audio

Enable input and feed it from the desk. It tracks four bands, picks out onsets, locks a beat clock, and flags breaks, drops and changes of sound. G free-runs the clock without unplugging anything.

Type

Drop a .otf / .ttf / .woff anywhere to use your own face.

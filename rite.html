Rite

Rite

The frame is cut into blocks. Each block holds one word set to its edges, a sigil, a field of colour, or nothing. A seed is a whole composition, so reseeding is a cut — and the music decides when to cut.

Motion

The six dials are routings, not parameters. Each one says how hard a part of the music drives a behaviour, so you wire the track to the frame once and then leave it alone.

Sigils

Draw a Sigil opens a surface over the stage. Anything you keep goes into the library and gets placed into blocks, where it scratches itself in over four beats. With an empty library the engine draws its own.

Audio

Enable input and feed it from the desk. It tracks four bands, picks out onsets, locks a beat clock to the track, and flags breaks, drops and changes of sound. With nothing connected the clock free-runs at 120.

Output

Any resolution, including LED strips. Fullscreen renders at the true output size on whichever display it is on; Output Window gives you a chromeless copy that stays in step.

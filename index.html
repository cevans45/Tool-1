TYPO

Typo

Live type lab for breaking text into shards, grids, and glitchy geometry.

Set a phrase, cycle filters that warp outlines and spacing, and drop in your own font files to see the same treatments on custom faces. Built for quick poster fragments and exportable stills.

SIGIL

Sigil

Mirror-symmetric ink drawing with a rough, mycelium-like line quality.

Draw on one side and watch the other side echo in real time. Tune stroke and fill, then export with a jagged edge for print or texture work.

PEARLS

Pearls

Pearl-like circles and clumps on a grid for fast compositional sketches.

Place nodes, grow soft blobs, randomize clusters, and nudge a layout before you commit to heavier design tools. Ideal for rhythm and negative-space studies.

PRISM

Prism

Full-canvas shader moods—kaleido weave, bloom, drift, and more.

Pick a preset, dial parameters, and let the GPU handle layered motion and color. Use it as a background generator or to grab frames for stills and video plates.

Refresh the seed to explore new line networks, then refine symmetry and density for logo-adjacent marks and UI ornament.
